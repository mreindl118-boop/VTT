## Changes

Converted from the official PDF to Markdown by the your5e/5e-srd-markdown project, which lists its own changes: it removed the table of contents; renumbered the steps of D20 Tests; moved tables and sidebars to where they are used and removed their titles and the sentences that named them (for example “as shown on the Travel Pace table. The table states” became “The table states”); changed “on [the] table” to “in [the] table”; gave tables with several header rows a single header row; restructured side-by-side tables; split tables at their mid-table headings; and laid out the monster ability tables as score, modifier and saving throw. mistLAB converted that Markdown to HTML and JSON, reformatted and indexed it, and, checked against the official PDF, restored the table titles (not the sentences that named them), rejoined words split by the PDF extraction, merged the Travel Pace header into one row, set the Efreeti Bottle table’s die back to 1d10 as printed, rejoined the licence URL on the legal page, corrected “wwizareapon” to “weapon” in the Thrown property, put back the hyphens the Markdown had dropped where the PDF breaks a line (foot-by, foot-high, foot-long, foot-square, foot-wide, long-dead, long-term, real-world, shape-shifting) and the 10 dashes it had dropped there, and removed stray italics around punctuation. Apart from formatting and these changes, no rules text was changed.
