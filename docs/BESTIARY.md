# Bestiary: every open monster as a 3D model

The owner asked for the Monster Manual as models, open-source models first and the rest built to the house standard, with a DM-facing stat block at a glance. The open subset is the monsters of SRD 5.1 (2014 rules) and SRD 5.2.1 (2024 rules), CC BY 4.0; monsters outside the SRD are out of scope. Curse of Strahd creatures keep their figures and show module quick numbers and page references only.

Decisions: 379 creatures (both SRDs plus the repository): 272 built by family generators, 54 use existing figures, 53 restyled from openly licensed models once each licence is verified on its official page (none is shipped until then; every family generator is built in full so the bestiary is complete with zero imports).

The zoom and nesting fixes this plan diagnosed (its cluster 0) are built separately and ship first.

## Build order

- 0b. After confirmation: cluster 0b-perf-baseline records e2e/perf-baseline.json on the current branch (after cluster 0) before any bestiary code lands.
- Wave 1 (parallel, disjoint files): 1-foundation-anatomy (including heads.ts shared kits, the register()/getFigure() stub and the validated cpalette), 2-import-pipeline (pipeline, decoder, offline precache and tests; model packs committed only for licence-verified sources), 3-registration-routing phase A (srd-index.json, repository entries, placedCreatureId, Token.form and Token.edition, routing to existing figures, realSize-gated multipliers, shared-geometry cache), 4-dm-statblock-tooltip (after cluster 0 merges; behind its adapter until the books lookup lands).
- Wave 2 (parallel after cluster 1 only; no wave-2 cluster depends on another): 5-people, 6-undead-spirits, 7-mammals, 8-hoofed-and-hybrids, 9-dragons-and-reptiles, 10-air-and-water, 11-crawlers-oozes-plants, 12-fiends-elementals-constructs. Each builds its procedural generators in full; imports are used only for ids where cluster 2 reports a verified pack and the family cohesion sheet passes.
- Wave 3: 3-registration-routing phase B, merged family by family as each lands (registry, lazy chunks, swarm members, form picker wiring, realSize allowlist shrinking), then the routing test with an empty allowlist and the multiplier tables deleted.
- Wave 4 (mandatory): 3-registration-routing phase C (shared merged geometry, InstancedMesh per material for repeated kinds, one shadow batch, ring batches), then the hard perf gate in e2e/bestiary-perf.spec.ts on desktop, phone and WebKit and the whole-bestiary contact sheet review.

## Budgets

Measured today (contact-sheet harness, 175 figures): 24 to 964 triangles (median 532). The humanoid is about 540 triangles and 5 draw calls after mergeByMaterial; figures merge to 1 to 9 draw calls (median 4).  Triangles per figure, counted before merging (floor-ceiling; the floor stops anything reading as a placeholder): - Tiny: 150-500. - Small: 300-900. - Medium: 500-1,500 (a humanoid plus a role prop: 600-900). - Large: 900-3,000. - Huge: 2,000-5,000. - Gargantuan: 3,000-8,000. - Floor exemption: primitive-shape creatures (will-o'-wisp orb, animated flying sword, the four oozes, any figure whose FIGURE_META.primitive is true) are exempt from the floor because the facet rule (icosahedron detail 0-1) caps them lower. They are never exempt from the ceiling.  Imports are decimated by facet size, not by triangle count: a target edge length of 0.4-0.6 ft at Medium scale (scaled linearly with the figure's height for other sizes), matched to the kit's facets. The result must also land inside the size band. Sources run from 1,248 to 4,920 triangles, and the legacy eagle is 24,800.  Materials and draw calls (the shadow and ring are counted, not hidden): - At most 6 materials per monster and 8 per named NPC; at most 2 of them translucent. - Contact shadows are NOT per-figure meshes. Each figure declares FIGURE_META.shadow {rx, rz}; all creature shadows on a level draw as one InstancedMesh (1 call in total). All base rings draw as one instanced batch per ring colour (1-2 calls in total). - So a figure's own draw calls after mergeByMaterial equal its material count, and the per-level overhead is fixed at about 3 calls. - No vertex colours, UVs, textures or normal maps: mergeByMaterial skips meshes that carry a colour attribute, so a figure with them costs one call per part.  Encounter gates (absolute, measured by e2e/bestiary-perf.spec.ts against e2e/perf-baseline.json, which cluster 0b records BEFORE wave 1): - Vallaki (ch05/N) plus 40 tokens (20 wolves + 20 mixed SRD figures, at least one Huge): 150 or fewer added draw calls, 60,000 or fewer added triangles, and 60 fps on an iPad-class GPU (WebKit project; frame time p95 16.7 ms or less on the reference desktop with 4x CPU throttle as the proxy). Measured on desktop Chromium, the phone project and WebKit. - Arithmetic check: without instancing each token costs its materials (up to 6) + 1 ring + 1 shadow, about 8 calls, so 40 tokens are about 320 calls, which FAILS the gate. Phase C is therefore mandatory, not optional. - With phase C: 40 tokens of about 15 distinct kinds at about 4 materials each is about 60 calls, + 1 shadow batch + 2 ring batches, well inside 150. - 20 identical figures: 6 or fewer draw calls for the figures (one InstancedMesh per material) + the shared shadow and ring batches. - GPU memory added by 40 placed figures: 20 MB or less (pitfall 3). Shared geometry: the merged figure is cached per (id, variant, form) and its BufferGeometry is shared by every token of that key; nothing is cloned and re-merged per token.  Build cost: a procedural figure builds in 5 ms or less (median over all ids in vitest); a cached figure costs 0 ms.  Imported model packs: - One .fig file per id: 40 KB or less (hard limit 64 KB). - All imported packs together: 2.5 MB or less. - Decoding every pack at boot on an iPad: 50 ms or less.  Code size: the app/src/kit/bestiary family modules are lazy chunks. Together they stay at 250 KB minified or less. They and the .fig files are precached from the Vite build manifest by sw.js (cluster 2), with the cache name bumped per build so a stale .fig never meets a newer decoder (pitfall 49).  Dimensions: - Each figure's bounding box is within ±15% of its DIMS entry. - minY is 0 ± 0.05, unless the creature is a declared hoverer or swimmer. - Large and bigger quadrupeds, serpents and blobs cover at least 80% of the space side. Exemption: worms emerging from the ground (purple worm), whose rubble ring fills the space instead. - Every new figure faces +z; kept figures that face +x (site goat, giant goat, elk, roc) get FIGURE_META.facingDeg.

## Import pipeline

The pipeline is offline only. It adds no runtime dependency, and no CDN is used at runtime. Code lives in scripts/models/import/**, output in app/public/models/**, and the runtime reader is app/src/kit/bestiary/pack.ts. Cluster 2 also owns package.json, package-lock.json, .gitignore, vite.config.ts and app/public/sw.js.  0. SOURCES AND LICENCE GATE (blocking; nothing is committed to app/public/models before it passes):    scripts/models/import/sources.json lists every source file. For each one it records:    - pack title, author, the official page URL (quaternius.com, or the author's own itch.io or poly.pizza listing), and the file's URL;    - licence (allowlist: CC0-1.0 and CC-BY-4.0 only; never NC, ND, 'editorial', unclear, AI-generated or ripped from a game);    - the evidence: a licence statement quoted from the official page, or the licence file shipped inside the official download, plus the licence URL;    - retrieval date and sha256 of the official download;    - status: 'verified' or 'unverified'.    fetch.mjs downloads into scripts/models/import/.cache (gitignored) and verifies the sha256. Any file without verified evidence stays 'unverified': restyle.mjs refuses to write its pack, and its creature ids build procedurally (every open family has a full procedural generator). Today ALL 21 files are unverified: the planning copies came from third-party GitHub mirrors, and the proxy returns CONNECT 403 for quaternius.com, poly.pizza, quaternius.itch.io, opengameart.org and creativecommons.org. The 2016-2018 legacy packs (Cat, Dog, Eagle, Piranha FBX) are the least certain.    Unblocking needs the user: either allow those hosts through the session proxy (see /root/.ccr/README.md), or supply the official zips; a human-recorded sources.json entry with the page URL, licence statement and sha256 is also acceptable.     Candidate sources (21 files, all claimed CC0 by the author, pending verification):    - Quaternius Animated Animals: Wolf, Fox, Husky, Horse, Horse_White, Donkey, Deer, Stag, Bull.    - Quaternius Animated Dinosaurs: Trex, Velociraptor, Triceratops.    - Quaternius Animated Fish: Shark, Dolphin.    - Quaternius Easy Enemy pack: Rat, Frog, Spider.    - Quaternius 2016-2018 free animal packs (FBX): Cat, Dog, Eagle, Piranha.    The big cats no longer use the Cat; it serves only the house cat.     Rejected on style:    - Quaternius Ultimate Monsters: chibi, googly eyes.    - KayKit Adventurers and Skeletons, and Kenney Mini Characters: chibi, texture atlases.    - Quaternius Modular Women, Knight, Animated Human, Zombie and Goblin: they would break the single shared humanoid grammar of STYLE.md.    - Legacy Bat and Dragon, and Easy Enemy Snake and Wasp: cartoon proportions.    - Cow, Alpaca, ShibaInu, Apatosaurus, Parasaurolophus, Stegosaurus, Fish1-3, Manta ray and Whale: no matching SRD creature.    - Pirate-kit tentacle: textured.  1. VARIANTS: scripts/models/import/variants.json has one entry per creature id that uses an open model (53 ids) and one per part donor (horse-lower, spider-lower, rat-head, cat-head; donors are optional upgrades, every consumer has a heads.ts or procedural fallback). Each entry gives:    - source file, animation clip and frame for the ready pose (perched or half-spread for birds);    - boneScales {bone: [x, y, z]} applied before baking (pony head 1.12, hyena hind legs 0.8, gorgon horns);    - duplicateParts (the death dog's second head, splayed ±18°) and removeParts (the donors);    - a part palette {part: cpalette token, surface name, emissive?}; hexes come only from cluster 1's cpalette.ts, which validates them;    - targetEdgeFt (0.4-0.6 ft at Medium, scaled with height) and the size band it must land in;    - DIMS reference and the anchors to export (head, jaw, neck, withers, hips, tailBase, each eye, each forefoot).  2. PARTS MAP: scripts/models/import/parts/<model>.json maps each bone name to a part (head, neck, torso, legs, tail, mane, horns, eyes, teeth, hooves or claws).  3. RESTYLE (restyle.mjs): runs inside Playwright Chromium (already a devDependency), using three's GLTFLoader and FBXLoader from node_modules, as the existing scratchpad harness does. Steps:    - refuse any source whose sources.json status is not 'verified';    - load the model and set the clip and frame;    - apply the bone scales, then bake the skinned positions on the CPU (SkinnedMesh.applyBoneTransform per vertex);    - assign each triangle to the part of its dominant bone weight;    - strip the rig, animations, UVs, normals, vertex colours (the Quaternius Deer has them), morph targets, textures and PBR materials;    - merge each part into one geometry and decimate it by edge collapse until the median edge length reaches targetEdgeFt, keeping silhouette edges first: meshoptimizer (MIT) added as an explicit devDependency in package.json and package-lock.json (today it is only an extraneous package in node_modules), or three/examples SimplifyModifier if meshoptimizer is not wanted;    - un-index the geometry and drop normals, so flatShading recomputes the facets;    - re-pivot: origin at the floor centre of the footprint, feet at y = 0, front turned to +z;    - scale to the DIMS entry in feet (not to a size category); the FBX files are in centimetres, scale 0.01 first;    - colour each part from its cpalette token; assert HSL saturation 0.35 or less unless the part is emissive. The legacy FBX files are untextured white, so their colours come only from the variant palette.  4. FORMAT: each id is written to app/public/models/<id>.fig, little-endian:    - header: 'MFIG', u16 version, u16 partCount, then the f32 bbox min and max;    - per part: u8 name length + name, u32 RGB colour, u8 surface-name length + surface NAME (a string such as 'fur', never a SURF ordinal, so cluster 1 can renumber SURF freely), u32 emissive (0 means none), u8 opacity, u32 vertex count, then non-indexed Int16 xyz positions quantised to the bbox;    - then u8 anchor count, each with a name and 3 x f32.    A 1,500-triangle figure is about 27 KB.  5. RUNTIME (pack.ts):    - loadFigures() fetches and decodes every .fig at boot, in parallel with the manifests, and dispatches 'mistlab:figures-ready'. Pack URLs carry ?v=<sha8 of the file> from a generated models/index.json.    - importedFigure(id, opts) is synchronous. It builds one mesh per part with mat(hex, {surface: SURF[name]}) (unknown names fall back to 'generic' and fail the unit test), or matClone plus setOpacity for translucent parts, so the coverage fog, world-space grain and mergeByMaterial all apply.    - anchorsOf(id) returns the exported anchors; hasFigure(id) is false for unverified or missing packs.    - Token builds wait for the packs for at most 3 s. After that they use the procedural generator of that id (not a legacy kit stand-in) and swap when 'figures-ready' arrives.  6. OFFLINE (sw.js, vite.config.ts): vite.config.ts enables build.manifest; a small build plugin writes the precache list (shell, every lazy family chunk, models/index.json and every .fig) into sw.js together with CACHE = 'mistlab-<build hash>'. The activate handler already deletes old caches, so bumping the name per build retires stale chunks and packs. The existing 'cache-pack' message keeps working for chapter packs.  7. KIT ADDITIONS: family modules attach procedural kit parts at the anchors, using the shared anatomy helpers: horns, wings, flames, barding, collars, mane, sabres.  8. REVIEW (blocking per family): scripts/models/sheet renders, per family, the raw import, the restyled figure and every procedural member of the same family side by side, at eye height and at 40 px, plus grey silhouettes. A restyled import must not read smoother, more detailed or more cartoon-proportioned than its procedural neighbours. If the reviewer cannot pass the family sheet after coarsening, the whole family switches to its procedural generator: one source per family, never a mix that reads as two styles.  9. REPRODUCIBILITY: 'restyle.mjs --check' rebuilds every verified pack and fails if any byte differs from the committed app/public/models. A unit test decodes every pack and asserts the budgets, the licence allowlist, status 'verified' with a sha256, and an entry in credits.json for each one.  10. CREDITS: app/public/models/credits.json and app/public/models/LICENSES/*.txt are written from sources.json, together with the changes made to each model. Until an entry is verified its credit line reads 'licence pending verification' and the model is not shipped.

## The DM stat-block tooltip

A DM-only creature card, built in app/src/ui/creature-card.ts and app/src/ui/creature-card.css, following the tier B rule-card language of docs/CLARITY.md: - opaque --card surface, min(320px, 100vw - 24px) wide; - a 4px header strip in the kind colour; - placed by placeCard(), docked as a bottom sheet below 480px; - dismissed by Esc, an outside tap or the X; a hover-opened card also closes on mouse leave after a 250 ms grace; - role=dialog with aria-labelledby; - one card at a time. If the tooltip manager from the clarity pass (ui/tooltip.ts and ui/rulecard.ts) has landed, the card registers there as the 'monster' renderer instead of keeping its own popover code.  TRIGGERS (every creature the DM sees, not only tokens): - Tokens: desktop hover over a creature token (app.describe hit, 300 ms delay); keyboard focus on a token-list entry, then Enter or Space. - Creatures placed directly in scenes (build.ts CREATURES kinds, hit via hit.object): bestiary.placedCreatureId(kind) (cluster 3) maps the placement kind to a bestiary id. On desktop, hover shows the card after 300 ms; on a tap, the glance block heads the existing object infocard (main.ts hit.object branch) in DM mode only. Kinds with no mapping show no card, never a guessed SRD card. - Creatures sheet rows (ui/bestiary.ts): hover, focus plus Enter or Space, or a tap on the row's name chip. - iPad and phone (no hover): the FIRST tap on a token or a placed creature shows the glance block at the top of the token panel or infocard, with no extra tap. Tapping the creature-name chip pins the full card. - Long-press stays reserved (CLARITY), so no long-press trigger.  AT A GLANCE (always visible, no scrolling on a 390x844 phone): - name, with the creature-type paw glyph; - type line in the kind colour, italic: '<Size> <type> (<subtype>) · <alignment>'; - key-stats grid: AC (with the armour note on hover or focus), HP as current/max from the token's hp {cur, max} (the max alone on a sheet row or a placed creature), with a thin bar; speed with every mode (walk, fly, swim, climb, burrow, hover); CR with XP (and PB in 2024); initiative in 2024; - a row of the six abilities: STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA, each score with its modifier, e.g. '16 (+3)'.  EXPANDED (a 'More' disclosure, remembered per viewer in a try/catch-wrapped localStorage key): - saving throws, skills, senses with passive Perception, languages; - damage vulnerabilities, resistances and immunities; condition immunities; gear (2024); - traits, actions, bonus actions, reactions and legendary actions as collapsible sections. Each section heading is a term chip; dice and DC text become DM-only roll buttons once increment 8 ships core/dice.ts. - For a creature with forms (the vampire and the five lycanthropes): a form picker that writes Token.form (cluster 3 schema) and so switches the token's figure.  DATA: - SRD creatures: lookup(name, edition, {type:'monster'}) from app/src/books/lookup.ts (books pass). The glance block comes from MonsterSummary (size, creatureType, alignment, ac, acNote, initiative, hp, hpDice, speed, cr, xp, pb, abilities, saves, skills, senses, languages, resistances, vulnerabilities, immunities, conditionImmunities, gear). The expanded sections come from the entry's html, split at its section headings. - Edition per token: a token stores Token.edition ('2014'\|'2024') from the Rules edition preference at the moment it is inserted, and its hp.max is that edition's HP (135 creatures changed HP between editions). The card opens in the token's edition. If the current Rules edition differs, the card shows a one-line note ('Inserted under 2014 rules; HP max 11') and a 'See 2024' toggle that swaps the text only; it never rewrites the token's HP. Sheet rows and placed creatures use the current Rules edition. A creature in only one edition carries a neutral '⚠ 2014 only' or '⚠ 2024 only' badge. The footer shows the edition badge ('2014 · SRD 5.1' / '2024 · SRD 5.2.1'), 'Open in Books ›' and the CC-BY attribution line from the entry. - One source per card: never merge lookup fields with bestiary.ts STATS. - Renamed repository ids are mapped through srd-index.json: acolyte→Priest Acolyte, thug→Tough, veteran→Warrior Veteran, cult-fanatic→Cultist Fanatic, flying-sword→Animated Flying Sword, rug-of-smothering→Animated Rug of Smothering, black-cat→Cat. - Curse of Strahd and other non-SRD creatures (banshee, revenant, poltergeist, flameskull, the blights, scarecrow, arcanaloth, nothic, death slaad, strahd-zombie, mongrelfolk, and so on): only the repository quick numbers (AC, HP, speed, CR from STATS), labelled 'Module stats', plus a module page reference ('Curse of Strahd, p. N' from an optional page field on the entry, or 'See Curse of Strahd, Appendix D'). Never book text, and no SRD-looking ability row unless the repository has the numbers. Nothing is ever shown for product-identity monsters that are not in the repository, and nothic and death slaad are excluded from srd-index.json and from SRD search. - Named NPCs: the person kind colour and glyph, quick numbers if the repository has them, and their SRD template's card when the manifest names one (for example Vistani bandit → Bandit).  COLOUR, matching the tooltip pass (CLARITY type palette): the header strip and type line use --k-monster (#9b1f33 light / #ff9aa8 dark). Creature type uses the same token with the paw glyph and the type word. Named people use --k-person with the bust glyph. The DM badge uses --k-dm with the eyeOff glyph. There are no new per-creature-type hues, because the tooltip pass gives creature types the monster token and tells kinds apart by glyph and type word. Colours come only from the CSS variables, in light and dark (prefers-color-scheme and data-theme). Reconcile with the books pass's TYPE_META --t-monster (#9b2335) so there is one token.  NEVER ON THE PLAYERS' SIDE: - The card and the glance block render only when mode is 'dm' and app.restricted is false; a player's tap on a placed creature keeps today's infocard with no stats. - The Player Display mounts no card code. - On any view flip (slider to Players, peek, mode switch) every open card closes. - No card HTML or stat value goes into channel or projection payloads. - Players keep seeing only seenAs() text and aliasFor() names, as today. HP numbers never appear on the players' side (pitfall 26). ## Risks

- LICENCE PROVENANCE (blocking for imports): no model can be verified from this session. The scratchpad copies came from third-party GitHub mirrors, and the proxy returns CONNECT 403 for quaternius.com, poly.pizza, quaternius.itch.io, opengameart.org and creativecommons.org. The 2016-2018 legacy packs (Cat, Dog, Eagle, Piranha) are the least certain. Mitigation: status 'unverified' blocks the pack, credits say 'pending verification', and every family builds its procedural generator in full, so the bestiary ships complete even with zero imports. Unblock: the user allows the hosts or supplies the official zips.
- PERFORMANCE ON IPAD: each token today is merged on its own (app.ts ~529), so a token costs its materials + a ring + a shadow, about 8 calls; 40 tokens on Vallaki would add about 320 calls, and per-token clones defeat geometry sharing. Mitigation: absolute gate (150 calls, 60k triangles, 60 fps) against a baseline recorded before wave 1; phase C instancing and the shadow and ring batches are mandatory. Lazy chunks, pack decode and translucent figures sorting with the mist and coverage fog can still regress the frame rate (pitfalls 1-3). WebKit may not be installable offline for Playwright; then the iPad number comes from a real device check before wave 4 closes.
- SIZE MULTIPLIERS: build.ts ~355 scales placed creatures (tiny 0.5, small 0.75) and bestiary.ts ~89 scales tokens (up to gargantuan 1.4). Removing them before real-size figures exist would rescale every tiny or small creature already on the Curse of Strahd maps. Mitigation: FIGURE_META.realSize gates the skip per id; legacy ids keep the multiplier; before-and-after sheets of Death House, the Amber Temple and Vallaki; tables deleted only when the allowlist is empty.
- EDITION DIFFERENCES: 27 size changes (for example, the invisible stalker and swarm of bats are Large in 2024), 135 HP changes, AC changes (the wolf is AC 13 in 2014 and AC 12 in 2024; 2024 is the default edition), and 'Medium or Small' NPCs. Mitigation: Token.edition and an edition-specific hp.max set at insertion, a note on the card when the toggle differs, the ring following the chosen edition and the DM's per-token override (pitfall 27), and edition-parametrised tests. 10 creatures are 2014-only and 22 are 2024-only.
- PALETTE: the earlier draft used non-emissive colours above HSL S 0.35 (the true dragons, the vampire cape, sores, eyes, the eagle beak). They are re-derived in the decisions table and published as validated cpalette tokens; family files may not contain hex literals. The metallic dragons still sit within a few degrees of hue (brass, bronze, gold), so the head kit is the required difference and a 40 px grey test gates cluster 9.
- COHESION OF IMPORTS: Quaternius animals are clean low-poly but can read smoother or more cartoon-like than the kit; the Easy Enemy rat and frog have large eyes; the legacy cat has domestic proportions (so the big cats are procedural). Triangle-count decimation does not reproduce the kit's facet scale. Mitigation: decimation to a target edge length, a blocking per-family side-by-side sheet, and one source per family (a failed gate switches the family to procedural).
- SKIN-BAKE ARTEFACTS: bone-scale edits and the death dog's duplicated head can tear or intersect after decimation. Edit before decimating, and check the 40 px and eye-height sheets.
- DEPENDENCY ON THE BOOKS PASS: app/src/books/lookup.ts exists only in worktree wf_646bc177-602-2 (its last commit is a WIP after an interrupted agent), so its API may still change. Also, its TYPE_META monster colour (#9b2335) differs from CLARITY's --k-monster (#9b1f33); reconcile to one token.
- SHARED-FILE CONFLICTS with other passes: main.ts (cluster 0 first, then cluster 4; the clarity pass also edits the token panel and tip routing), styles.css (cluster 0), render/materials.ts and kit/surfaces.ts (the stylization pass), render/build.ts and app.ts (cluster 0's buildContext change and cluster 3 phases A and C), app/public/sw.js and vite.config.ts (cluster 2), state/campaign.ts (cluster 3; the gameplay increments also touch it), and humanoid() (the stylization pass keeps it as the base). Sequence the merges and keep each edit to the named lines.
- STYLIZATION PASS NOT FINISHED: its run journal (wf_9e5d7fe1-168) contains surveys only; its agents also pivoted to the zoom report, and no creature design (five stylistic and five realism improvements) exists yet. Align cluster 1's pose presets, shadow batch and ring colour with that design when it lands, so there are not two treatments.
