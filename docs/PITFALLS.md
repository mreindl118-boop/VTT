# Pitfalls other tabletops hit, and the rules that keep mistLAB out of them

Gathered from reviews, forum threads, issue trackers and store pages. Each rule says where it applies in mistLAB and how it is checked. Every increment in `docs/UPGRADES.md` lists the pitfalls it guards.

| # | Pitfall | Evidence | Our rule | Where it bites and how it is checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lighting, vision and overlays drag performance down until the table stalls | Roll20 UDL lag and 3 s token updates; AboveVTT cannot run more than a few lights; in TaleSpire one torch costs up to 10 fps. [1](https://app.roll20.net/forum/post/10705870/new-dynamic-lighting-lags-horribly) [2](https://github.com/cyruzzo/AboveVTT/issues/899) [3](https://steamcommunity.com/app/720620/discussions/0/4358995579927661319/) | Every increment passes the committed per-scene baseline on both desktop and phone projects. Coverage is cached per level and recomputed only on commits. Overlays are screen-space or merged meshes. | Increment 2 creates e2e/perf.spec.ts and perf-baseline.json, gated on desktop and phone (+20% calls or triangles, +30% load, median of 3). Increments 5, 6, 9, 10 and 11 must keep it green; the spec fails the build if not. |
| 2 | Testing only on Chromium while the target is iPad Safari | Foundry #9982: lag appears only in Firefox and was closed as not reproducible; Apple forum WebKit context-loss threads. [1](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/9982) [2](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/778735) | A WebKit Playwright project runs the smoke and perf suites, and every release records a manual iPad measurement. | Increment 2 adds the webkit project (ui and perf specs; load gated at +50%) and docs/PERF-IPAD.md. A release is blocked when an iPad row is more than 30% worse than the previous release. |
| 3 | iPad Safari kills WebGL contexts under GPU memory pressure | Apple forums report the GPU process killed at roughly 300-500 MB. [1](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/735018) | One WebGL context per window, a capped DPR, and context-loss rebuild from state. | Increment 2 handler: the e2e calls WEBGL_lose_context. Increment 6 cards use colour chips. Increment 12 snapshots and print reuse the main renderer; canvas count and webglcontextlost events are asserted. |
| 4 | Client-side trust: the players' window receives the full DM state | In TaleSpire #531 a player could see everything the GM had; WVTT filters before sending. [1](https://github.com/Bouncyrock/TaleSpire-Beta-Public-Issue-Tracker/issues/531) [2](https://github.com/mastervash/wvtt) | channel.ts sends only projectState(state). Every CampaignState key is classified 'player' or 'dm', and an unclassified key fails the build. | Increment 1 adds the projection and the classification test. Increments 7 (notes, taken), 8 (statOverrides, rolls), 9, 12, 13 and 14 (tarokka, spies, reminders) each classify their new keys. An e2e listener on the display asserts that received states contain no 'dm' keys. |
| 5 | Fog and state desync between the GM and the player view | Foundry #3140 and #2125; Roll20 AFoW reset needed a reload; DDB spectator-view fog failures. [1](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/3140) [2](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/2125) | Reveals live only in CampaignState and change through commit(). The display renders the last full projected state, carried with sequence numbers. The only display-local state is an explicit Freeze. | Increments 4 and 5. e2e/display.spec.ts compares DM t=0 and display pixels (under 0.5% differing) after a reveal, a re-fog, an undo, a display reload and a resize. |
| 6 | Per-user fog diverges and the GM cannot see the players' actual view | Foundry #4041, open since 2020. [1](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/4041) | The slider at t=0 and the display are pixel-equal, with one exploration record per level. | Increment 5 adds the pixel comparison to display.spec.ts. |
| 7 | Vision leaks through geometry | Foundry #4850 and #12971; FGU guidance that doors must sit flush. [1](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/4850) [2](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/12971) | Visibility comes from authored rooms and door states. Every door lies on a wall and every room is closed. | Increment 4 adds the 'room open to' warning, plus closed-room and door-on-wall assertions in tests/manifests.test.ts. Increment 11 improvised scenes must pass the same test. |
| 8 | Secrets betrayed by rendering: silhouettes, markers, map edges | mistLAB bugs 6 and 15; Owlbear Fill Fog. [1](https://docs.owlbear.rodeo/docs/fog/) | Unexplored shell geometry is not drawn on the players' side, and secret doors are pixel-identical to wall. | The shader change is approval A3. Increment 12 asserts the players' picture is pixel-equal to the fog colour over unrevealed rooms. Increment 5 checks every display frame during a load with the curtain on, using the __mist.onFrame test hook. |
| 9 | Hidden names and positions leak through side channels | mistLAB bug 4; Roll20 turn outline; Foundry #8265; TaleSpire #865 and #798. [1](https://app.roll20.net/forum/post/12253465/turn-order-yellow-outline-showing-for-players-on-gm-layer-hidden-tokens) [2](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/8265) [3](https://github.com/Bouncyrock/TaleSpire-Beta-Public-Issue-Tracker/issues/798) | Every players'-side payload is built by core/playersView.ts. Hidden tokens contribute nothing. Features that add combatants or turn-bar text on the players' side require approval A2 to have shipped. | Increment 6 adds the payload fuzz tests. Increments 8 (roll all foes), 9 (pips, ring, banner) and 11 (start encounter) are gated on A2 by a build flag, and tests/player-safety.test.ts asserts the players' turn bar has no hidden combatant before the flag can be set. |
| 10 | Showing a picture or card drags the secret along | Roll20 bio threads; EncounterPlus mirrored-iPad review. [1](https://app.roll20.net/forum/post/6856662/gm-showing-a-pic-of-a-monster-but-not-bio-to-players) [2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/encounter-virtual-tabletop/id1170693487) | Payloads are whitelisted, and the display never mirrors the DM screen. | Increments 6, 12, 13 (recap) and 14 (reading): unit tests feed states with DM notes and hidden ids and assert none appear. |
| 11 | Visibility inherited from another object's permission | Foundry journal pins inherit permission; reports that Show Players changes permission. [1](https://github.com/foundry-vtt-community/wiki/blob/main/Journal.md) [2](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/14722) | Each handout, card and NPC name has an explicit visibility state. Links never imply visibility. | Increment 12: linking a hidden handout to a revealed key leaves it hidden (unit and e2e). Increment 13: an NPC's real name reaches players only through Identify. |
| 12 | Overlays on the table screen that cannot be dismissed | Monk's Common Display #98, #114 and #109. [1](https://github.com/ironmonk88/monks-common-display/issues?q=is%3Aissue) | Overlays are held state with Clear, auto-clear and a 'Showing' chip. A player tap never dismisses one. | Increments 5, 6 and 12. The e2e asserts Clear still works after a display reload and that a tap leaves the overlay. |
| 13 | Pings, pointers and captions shown at the wrong moment | Dungeon Master Map Tool shows the pointer only on the live map; Foundry #10456 fires a ping from a drag. [1](https://github.com/Bowtie8904/DungeonMasterMapTool) [2](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues?q=is%3Aissue+ping) | Pings and live overlays are suppressed while the display is curtained, frozen or showing a handout, and they come only from the Pointer tool. | Increment 6 (e2e: a ping while frozen draws nothing; after Unfreeze, nothing is replayed) and increment 12 (no ping while a handout is shown). |
| 14 | Accidental reveals and destructive mass actions without undo | DDB Reveal All; Foundry #10874; TaleSpire #894. [1](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/d-d-beyond-feedback/216288-maps-feedback-feature-request-undo) [2](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/10874) | Mass fog actions are reveal-log entries, so the persisted Undo reveal covers them. | 15 | Replacing a shipped behaviour while calling it additive | The critic found that undo, label taps and measure were silently replaced in the first plan. [1](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/12254) | An increment may add buttons, options and tools; it may not change what an existing button, gesture or key does. Changes of that kind go to approvals. | Increment 4 keeps Undo reveal's persisted semantics (e2e: reveal, reload, undo). Increment 7 keeps '/' Enter jumping. Increment 10 keeps two-tap measure. Each is checked by the existing ui and move specs running unchanged. |
| 16 | Players' input can undo or leave DM state | Foundry #10777. [1](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/10777) | Undo, shortcuts and history are DM-only. | Increment 4: the e2e presses Ctrl-Z and arrow keys in Players view and on the display and asserts state is unchanged. |
| 17 | Hotkey collisions: one key doing two things, or shortcuts firing while typing | TaleSpire #894 and #934. [1](https://github.com/Bouncyrock/TaleSpire-Beta-Public-Issue-Tracker/issues/934) [2](https://github.com/Bouncyrock/TaleSpire-Beta-Public-Issue-Tracker/issues/894) | One KEYMAP table with unique bindings. Increment 4 (Ctrl-Z, arrows), increment 6 (pointer hold) and increment 7 (notes) each add an e2e: typing 'z' with Ctrl in a note textarea leaves the party position unchanged. |
| 18 | Mass fog operations that sweep secrets or doors along | Foundry #9696. [1](https://github.com/foundryvtt/foundryvtt/issues/9696) | Area reveals never include secret doors, hidden objects, slots or door state. | Increment 4 unit test. |
| 19 | Status icons too small, too many, or covering labels | Illandril HUD scaler; Foundry #11231.
